Privacy Policy

How A&J Plumbing & Solar collects, uses and protects your personal information, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), Act 4 of 2013.

Last updated: June 2026

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website, why we collect it, how we look after it, and the rights you have over it. By using our contact form you confirm that you have read and understood this policy.

1. Who is responsible for your information

A&J Plumbing & Solar is a plumbing and solar services business based in Kroonstad, Free State, and operated by Jacques du Plessis. For the purposes of POPIA, A&J Plumbing & Solar is the "responsible party" for the personal information collected through this website.

2. What personal information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us through our contact form. That is:

  • Name (required)
  • Phone number (required)
  • Email address (optional)
  • Company name (optional)
  • The service you select and the message you write (required)

We do not use cookies, advertising pixels, analytics scripts or any other tracking technology on this website. If you simply browse the site without submitting the form, we do not collect any personal information about you.

3. Why we collect it and our legal basis

We use the information you submit for one purpose only: to respond to your enquiry, answer your questions and, where relevant, arrange a quote or a site visit for solar or plumbing work.

Our legal basis for processing this information is your consent, which you give when you complete and submit the contact form. We do not use your information for marketing, and we do not build profiles about you.

4. Is providing your information voluntary?

Yes. Completing the contact form is entirely voluntary. However, the required fields (name, phone number, service and message) are the minimum we need in order to understand and respond to your request. If you prefer not to use the form, you are welcome to contact us directly by phone, WhatsApp or email instead.

5. Who we share it with, and cross-border transfer

Your contact form submission is sent directly from your browser to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), an independent third-party form-processing service. FormSubmit forwards your message to our business email inbox and does not use it for its own purposes.

FormSubmit processes data on servers located outside South Africa (in the United States). This means that, by submitting the form, your information is transferred across South Africa's borders. As permitted under section 72 of POPIA, you consent to this transfer when you submit the form. We do not sell, rent or otherwise share your personal information with any other third party, and we will never disclose it except where required to do so by law.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry information only for as long as we need it to deal with your request and to keep reasonable records of the work or quotes that follow from it. When it is no longer needed for these purposes, we delete it. You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time (see your rights below).

7. How we protect your information

This website is served over a secure, encrypted (HTTPS) connection, so the information you send through the form is protected in transit. We limit access to enquiry emails to the people who need them to respond to you, and we take reasonable steps to keep your information safe from loss, misuse or unauthorised access.

8. Your rights under POPIA

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Ask us to delete or destroy your information;
  • Object to our processing of your information; and
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing that already took place before you withdrew it).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Information Officer using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.
