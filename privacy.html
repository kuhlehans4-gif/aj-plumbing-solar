Privacy Policy

How A&J Plumbing & Solar handles your personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), Act 4 of 2013.

Who we are

A&J Plumbing & Solar is a South African business operated by Jacques du Plessis, based at 4 Blignaut Street, Kroonstad, Free State, 9499. We provide solar and plumbing services to homes and businesses in the Kroonstad area.

Contact: 082 828 5896 · info@ajplumbing.co.za

What information we collect

When you submit a service enquiry through our contact form we collect:

  • Name (required)
  • Phone number (required)
  • Email address (optional, only if you provide it)
  • Company name (optional, only if you provide it)
  • Description of your service request (required)

We do not use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts on this website. No personal information is collected from visitors who do not submit the form.

Why we collect it

We collect your contact details solely to respond to your enquiry and, where necessary, to arrange a quote or site visit. We will not use your information for any other purpose without your separate consent.

How your information is processed and stored

Enquiry submissions are forwarded to our email inbox via Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a third-party form-processing service. Web3Forms processes data on servers outside South Africa. By submitting the contact form you acknowledge this transfer of your information across South Africa's borders, as permitted under POPIA §72 where the recipient is subject to substantially similar data-protection obligations.

We retain contact information for a maximum of 12 months after the last communication with you, after which it is deleted from our records.

We do not sell, share, or otherwise disclose your personal information to any other third party.

Your rights under POPIA

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act you have the right to:

  • Be informed about what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion or destruction of your information
  • Object to the processing of your information
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (this will not affect processing carried out before withdrawal)

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Information Officer (details below). We will respond within a reasonable time and no later than as required by applicable law.
