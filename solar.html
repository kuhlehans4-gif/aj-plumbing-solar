Solar questions

Can solar help with load-shedding?

Yes, when the system includes the right inverter, battery capacity and essential circuits. A&J can discuss which parts of the property should stay powered.

Is a solar geyser different from solar backup power?

Yes. A solar geyser focuses on hot water, while backup power uses components such as panels, an inverter and batteries to support selected electrical loads.

What should I share when enquiring?

Share the property type, current geyser or power problem, what you want supported during interruptions and whether you already have any solar equipment installed.
